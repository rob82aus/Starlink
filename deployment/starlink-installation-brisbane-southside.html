COMMON DIY PITFALLS

Why Get Starlink Professionally Installed?

Plenty of people install the kit themselves and it goes fine. The ones who call us usually hit one of these common and costly stumbling blocks:

🌳Signal Issue

Partial Obstructions & Frequent Dropouts

The dish went up in a spot with partial obstructions from trees or rooflines, causing the connection to drop out every few minutes during video calls or gaming.

💧Property Risk

Crushed Cables & Water Leaks

The cable was jammed through a window frame or door seal and got damaged by weather, or unsealed roof penetrations allowed Queensland rainwater into the ceiling.

Climbing a steep, multi-storey or slippery metal/tile roof without proper harness safety turned out to be far more dangerous than expected.

📉Coverage Failure

WiFi Only Works in One Room

The router is stuck where the cable entered, leaving bedrooms, offices, and downstairs living areas in persistent dead zones with sluggish speeds.

📦Configuration

Complex Setup & Hardware Confusion

The Starlink kit arrived, but bypassing existing modems, configuring custom network switches, or troubleshooting app errors proved too frustrating.

Starlink in Brisbane & Regional Queensland

Starlink delivers high-speed, low-latency internet from a network of low Earth orbit satellites, with typical download speeds well above 100 Mbps and latency around 25 to 30 ms. For rural and semi-rural properties around Brisbane, Logan, Ipswich and the Scenic Rim where NBN fixed line never arrived, it’s often the fastest option available. It’s also a strong backup connection for businesses that can’t afford downtime.

As at mid-2026, Starlink residential plans in Australia start from around $75 per month, with hardware available as a free rental (you pay shipping) or an outright purchase. Roam plans suit caravans and 4WD setups. Pricing and promotions change regularly, so check starlink.com/au for current offers at your address.

HONEST LOCAL ADVICE

Is Starlink Right for Your Property?

If you have full Fibre to the Premises (FTTP), NBN is usually the better and cheaper option. Starlink is typically a major upgrade in these 4 scenarios:

📡Massive Upgrade

On Sky Muster Satellite

Tired of strict peak data allowances and 600ms+ high latency? Starlink delivers uncapped residential data with crisp 25–30ms response times for Zoom and online calls.

📶Speed Boost

Congested NBN Fixed Wireless

If your local fixed wireless tower slows to a crawl during evening peak hours, Starlink bypasses local tower congestion entirely via satellite mesh.

🚫True Broadband

No NBN Available at Your Address

For newly built acreages, semi-rural properties, and off-grid locations where NBN fixed line was never built, Starlink delivers full fiber-class speeds immediately.

💼Failover Shield

Business Redundancy & Zero Downtime

Businesses that cannot afford POS or server downtime use Starlink as an independent, secondary backup internet pipeline alongside existing fiber.
